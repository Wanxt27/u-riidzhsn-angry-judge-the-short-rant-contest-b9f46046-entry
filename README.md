A make.fun entry for [Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046).

`main.ts` (Node 22+, stdlib only) writes one short rant — 334 characters, a single paragraph — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The printer worked fine all week. Then, ten minutes before the deadline, it ate my report, flashed PAPER JAM, and beeped like it was laughing at me. I opened every tray. I begged. I threatened it. Nothing. So I printed it at the library, stapled it by hand, and walked in five minutes late holding the pages like a hostage negotiator.

One concrete annoyance — a printer that only jams when the deadline is real — escalated fast: the jam, the beep like laughter, the begging and threats, and the punch lands in the last line, walking in late holding the pages like a hostage negotiator. Furious but funny, every sentence earning its place.

Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```
